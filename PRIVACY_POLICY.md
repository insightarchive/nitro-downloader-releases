# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: September 2026_

NITRO Downloader ("the App", "we", "us") is a Windows desktop application.
This policy explains what data the App and its companion browser extension
handle.

## What we collect

- **License activation**: if you purchase a license, your email address and
  a device identifier are sent to our payment processor, Lemon Squeezy, to
  activate and track your license seats. We do not receive or store your
  payment details (card numbers, etc.) — Lemon Squeezy handles all payment
  processing directly.
- **Local data only**: your download history, settings, and queue are
  stored entirely on your own computer (in your local `%APPDATA%` folder).
  None of this is sent to us or to any third party.
- **Crash reports**: if the App crashes, a local crash log is saved on your
  computer so you can review it. It is never uploaded automatically.
- **Browser extension**: the extension reads page content only to detect
  downloadable media (e.g. video links) on pages you visit, and passes that
  information locally to the desktop App. It does not transmit your
  browsing activity to us or to any third-party server.

## What we don't do

- We do not sell or share your data with third parties.
- We do not track your usage or browsing activity outside of what's needed,
  locally, to detect downloadable content.
- We do not use analytics or advertising trackers in the App.

## Third-party services

- **Lemon Squeezy** processes payments and license activation. Their own
  privacy policy applies to the payment data they collect:
  https://www.lemonsqueezy.com/privacy
- Direct file downloads are made straight from the source you link to
  (e.g. a video site) — that site's own privacy policy applies to your
  interaction with it.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to support@nitrodownloader.app.

## Changes

We may update this policy as the App changes. The "Last updated" date above
will reflect the latest revision.
