# Refund Policy

_Last updated: September 2026_

We want you to be happy with your NITRO Downloader purchase.

## 14-day money-back guarantee

If you're not satisfied with your purchase, contact us within **14 days** of
your purchase date at support@nitrodownloader.app with your order email or
license key, and we'll issue a full refund — no questions asked.

## How refunds work

- Refunds are processed through Lemon Squeezy, our payment processor, back
  to your original payment method.
- Once a refund is issued, the associated license key is deactivated.
- Refund requests after the 14-day window are considered on a case-by-case
  basis (e.g. a genuine product defect we couldn't resolve).

## Contact

To request a refund, email support@nitrodownloader.app.
